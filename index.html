Frequently Asked Questions

What does it cost to use Scott DeGroot as my broker?

There is no cost to enroll or consult with Scott. Insurance carriers pay a standard broker commission set by the federal marketplace, meaning you receive professional guidance and ongoing support at absolutely zero additional charge to you.

How do I know if I qualify for financial subsidies?

Subsidies are based on your household size and estimated modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) for the tax year. You can instantly calculate your exact credit amount by entering your details into our secure HealthSherpa portal link.

Can I enroll outside of the Open Enrollment Period?

Generally, ACA enrollments take place during the annual Open Enrollment Period. However, if you experience a Qualifying Life Event (such as loss of job-based coverage, marriage, birth of a child, or moving), you may qualify for a Special Enrollment Period (SEP).

How do I get in touch with Scott directly?

You can call Scott directly at 803-851-3598 or use the online self-enrollment portal anytime to compare plans instantly.
